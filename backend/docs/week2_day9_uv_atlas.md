## 1. Architectural Scope
Day 9 delivers the core 3D mesh processing requirements of the Infotact Solutions specification:
1. **Least Squares Conformal Mapping (LSCM) UV Parameterization**: Flatten complex 3D meshes onto 2D plane with minimal angular distortion.
2. **2D Atlas Packing**: Layout charts in a unified unit square texture atlas with margin gutters preventing mipmap bleeding.
3. **Mid-Project Review Geometry & Rendering Audit**: Automated verification ensuring models are structurally sound (non-blob) and Three.js-renderable.
